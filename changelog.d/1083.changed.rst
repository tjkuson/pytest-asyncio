Each async fixture and each test now runs in an asyncio task of its own, named after the fixture or test; the task of an async generator fixture stays alive across the fixture's ``yield``, so that the task entering a task group, cancel scope or timeout before the ``yield`` is the task exiting it. pytest-asyncio manages internal tasks that may appear in ``asyncio.all_tasks()``; they are not part of its public API, and their names, number and arrangement may change between releases. Context variables behave as before.

A cancellation of a fixture's task while the fixture is in use, for example because a task in a task group spanning its ``yield`` failed, cancels the test or fixture setup running at the time and ends the normal work of that loop: fixture teardowns still run, so that the scope can exit and report the failure, but further tests and fixture setups in the loop are refused with an ``asyncio.CancelledError`` explaining why. A cancelled fixture setup or teardown is reported as ``PytestAsyncioError``. An interrupted test or fixture (Ctrl-C, or the signal of a timeout plugin) is cancelled and run to its end before pytest tears fixtures down.
